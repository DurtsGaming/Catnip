# Catnip design

Text summary of [design.pdf](design.pdf) ("Feral Forever: Resource Manager / Rotation Addon") and [layout-sketch.svg](layout-sketch.svg). The originals are the source of truth for visuals; this file is the working spec.

## Concept

A compact, circular Feral Druid HUD, ported from a WeakAuras setup. Inspiration: [Rabble's energy WA](https://wago.io/Ak7NxBdCc) plus the same author's Rage and Mana WAs ([wago.io/p/Rabble](https://wago.io/p/Rabble)).

Layout, from the centre out: the resource circle (with the GCD pie over it), the swing timer ring, then five combo point dots along an arc above, with the Rake ring around dot 4 and the Rip ring around dot 5. Clearcasting lights a crescent of light inside the top of the resource circle. The whole HUD draws at 0.85 alpha by default (General → Opacity); the cooldown box has its own Opacity slider (Cooldown → Layout, default 100%). Active icons ignore it and always draw at full strength: below 100% the grey cooldown icon underneath showed through them, and we can't hide that one in combat (2026-10-03).

- **Combo points follow the arc** of the big circle's top edge (a paw-print shape), not a flat row. See the sketch.
- Ignore the thin inner ring in the reference WA — it's the old Classic 2s energy ticker.

## MVP

1. **Resource circle (#1 on #2, bordered by #3).** Energy in Cat, Rage in Bear. Fills from the bottom; raw value as text in the middle.
2. **Combo points (#1 bordered by #3).** Five small circles; fill by count, appearing instantly (no fade-in). Hide the borders when not in Cat or Bear Form (shown in Bear so leftover points and DoTs stay visible).
3. **DoT rings (#5, #6).** Rip is a segmented red ring (~5.75px band, overlapping the dot's border ring) around the 5th combo point in 6 segments (12s, one per 2s tick), and Rake around the 4th in 3 (9s, one per 3s tick); one AuraContainer each, hidden with the dots outside Cat and Bear Form. Same colour for both, from their icons: dark crimson through red to hot orange-red, clockwise from 12 o'clock; 2-unit gaps like Faerie Fire's. Full when applied, drains clockwise to empty, leaving nothing behind. The timing is secret in combat, so unlike Faerie Fire there are no per-segment pulses, and the segments are cut into the texture. (A plain red ring came first; a thin red layer outside the swing ring was tried and dropped.)
4. **Swing timer (#7 on #4).** Ring around the big circle that appears full on each swing and empties clockwise from 12 o'clock (earlier versions filled, then emptied counter-clockwise), over the soft glow (#4, pulled slightly inside the bar's band) with a small gap from the resource circle. (A crisp black outline was tried and dropped in favour of the feathered look.) Turns red/pink when Maul is queued. The `0.2 / 1.0` text was tried and dropped: the ring alone is the swing timer.
5. **Enrage tint.** In Bear Form, while the Enrage buff is up, the empty part of the resource circle turns a dim red (a red disc behind the rage fill).
6. **Clearcasting proc texture.** A crescent of light: a thin bright arc just inside the top rim (barely dims) over a glow with soft rays spilling down into the fill (breathes), both drawn additively so they read as light, over a steady soft black backing (40%) and a thin dark line just under the arc (60%) so they still have contrast on a full energy fill (additive white alone barely changes bright yellow). Picked from five mockups (crescent, moonbeams, rim light, halo, sheen). (Mirrored claw marks were used first, then an opaque white cap over the top 10%, which looked like a sticker.) (A translucent "phantom" +10 energy preview was tried and removed.)

7. **Shifting Power cooldown.** A four-segment arc under the swing ring, centred on 6 o'clock, filling left to right over the cooldown (16s, or 12s/8s with talents, so each segment is a quarter: 4s/3s/2s, filling edge to edge over its own share). Colours by segment: blue (the Forever mana bar, empty to full end), blue to white, white to yellow, yellow (the energy bar's bright end), so segments 2 and 3 meet in white. Each segment has a thin black outline (1.5 units, just outside it), like the combo points, and unfilled parts show a 30% copy of their colour (`track`). The earlier outline in the segment's own colours, unfilled parts clear, read as a neon glow next to the rest of the HUD (2026-10-04; a black outline with filled dark slots had been tried before that and dropped). 4 units between segments, leaving 1 clear between outlines (was 6.5; closed up 2026-10-04). Each of the first three segments gives a small pulse (additive brighten to 55%, 0.08s up, 0.35s down) the moment it fills, e.g. at 4s, 8s and 12s of 16s; the fourth gets the ready flash instead. At zero the arc flashes and fades out (0.45s) and a blue light (brightened mana blue) along the inside of the resource circle's rim pulses once, over a 30% blue wash across the whole circle: 0.01s up, 0.5s down (shortened from 0.15s up, 1.2s down, which felt slow; a faint glow breathing while ready was tried and dropped). The blue light only shows if your mana pays for a cast (in any form); if it doesn't, there's no flash, and none later when the mana arrives (the orbs show that). Nothing shows if the spell isn't known. Picked over a cap over the top of the circle (covered the fill, and zero was hard to see), rim gem, halo, lit border, pips and lit number (mockup history in the 2026-10-03 session).
8. **Shifting Power mana counter.** Cat Form only, and only if Shifting Power is known. Shows in and out of combat for now (a combat-only version was tried; `COMBAT_ONLY` in `ShiftingPowerMana.lua` turns it back on) while the owner decides. A centred row of small orbs (60% of a combo point, blue-to-yellow diagonal blend with the combo points' black rim) under the Shifting Power arc, one per cast your mana pays for: floor(mana / cost), up to 5. Hidden while casting or channelling (spells and item uses), because the cast text sits in the same spot. The sketch's 0.1s fade-out/fade-in when the count changes isn't possible: in combat the count is secret, so we can't tell it changed, and the rows swap instantly. The original sketch put it under the swing timer text, which was dropped, so it sits under the arc instead.
9. **Faerie Fire cooldown.** A segmented ring around combo point 1 (same size and band as the DoT rings), starting full and emptying clockwise from 12 o'clock over Faerie Fire (Feral)'s 6s cooldown, leaving nothing behind (no outline or dim track), after a cast in Cat or Bear Form (the caster version has no cooldown). 6 segments (1s each; `segments` in `CooldownRings.lua`, any number from 3 up), 2 units apart (3 looked wide at six segments). Colours from the spell icon, deep violet through magenta to pale pink around the ring. As each segment empties the next one pulses, except the last one left (pulsing the emptied spot was tried first); at zero it's simply gone: no flash, no resource pulse (a whole-ring flash and fade was tried and dropped). (Filling with empty segments at 30% was tried first.) Hidden with the combo dots outside Cat and Bear Form. First of the short cooldowns tracked like DoTs (see Later).
10. **Growl and Primal Bite cooldowns.** Rings like Faerie Fire's (#9), Growl around combo point 2 and Primal Bite around combo point 3, coloured from their icons but kept distinct from Rake and Rip's red: Growl ember orange through amber to pale gold (the icon's fire), Primal Bite warm bone through ivory to near white (the teeth). Growl in 8 segments (about 8s, 1s each: Classic's 10s ran a segment and a half long in-game, 2026-10-04), Primal Bite in 6 (length unknown, 6s guessed). The real lengths are learned from the first cast: out of combat from the cooldown's numbers, in combat from when the game's ready flag turns on (the ring also ends there if it's running long).
11. **Stealth mode ("Moonlit").** While stealthed (Prowl, or Shadowmeld), the HUD cools down: the energy fill and combo points turn periwinkle (same shading as energy: deep indigo-violet at the bottom paling to lavender-white at the top, darker at both sides), the fill (only, not the border or number) dims to 70%, the outlines stay black, the number pales slightly and the Shifting Power orbs dim to 60% (30% was too much, 2026-10-04). The energy fill crossfades both ways: a copy of the old colour over the new fill fades out over 0.35s while the fill's alpha eases to its new value (an instant swap felt abrupt, 2026-10-04); the rest changes instantly. Picked over ghost outline, eclipse, paw print, shadow veil and core-only looks, then periwinkle over cool lemon, lime, jade, seafoam, glacier and pale gold (2026-10-04 mockups). The whole HUD dimming (0.85 to 0.75 in the mockup) was left out. The mockup's silver-blue outlines (resource border 60%, combo points 38%) were built and dropped: black turning pale looked bad in-game (2026-10-04). So did lightening the swing ring's dark glow (75% to 35% black), also dropped.

    **Shadow smoke.** We don't swing while stealthed, so the swing timer's black glow fades out (0.6s) and a smoke ring fades in (0.9s) in its place, centred on the swing band (61 units out, clear of the Shifting Power arc): a still dark-violet base (#1a0d2e, 70%) under two broken, blurred rings in the Prowl icon's colours that turn at different speeds, so their overlaps keep shifting: violet (#5b3496, 85%) clockwise once per 46s, lilac (#9b6fdc, 50%) counter-clockwise per 31s. (A third layer of sparse teal flecks, clockwise per 70s, was dropped after an in-game look.) Picked from ten mockups (starlit aurora, moonlit rim, constellation, ripples, rune circle, glint, bead wave, opal, shadowflame; earlier stalking moon, paw trail, starlit motes, cat's eyes, aurora) on 2026-10-04.

## Later

- ~~Mana (as %) in caster/other forms~~ done: shown as a % whenever the power is mana (via `UnitPowerPercent`). Lacerate stacks still to do.
- ~~Mana cost prediction~~ built (requested 2026-10-04, like Blizzard's player frame): while a spell with a cast time and a mana cost is cast, the mana fill drops by the cost and a darker band (the mana fill, dimmed) shows the part about to be spent. If the cast is cancelled, interrupted or fails, the fill climbs back up straight away at a steady rate over 0.5s, uncovering a glowing band: the mana fill with Blizzard's gain glow (`UI-StatusBar-Glow`, additive, 75%) on top, as Blizzard's player frame does. (First version, 2026-10-04: waited 0.1s, eased out and used a flat pale band that faded; side by side with Blizzard's frame it lagged, rushed and looked flat.) If it lands, the band goes when the mana is spent, with no dip. Only when the power shown is mana (not Cat or Bear Form; a heal pressed in Cat Form, which drops the form, shows it too: it used to show only sometimes, depending on which event came first, fixed 2026-10-04), and not while Clearcasting is known to be up (the spell is free). Instant casts and channels show nothing. The % number stays the current mana.
- ~~Five-second rule~~ built: in every form (mana keeps its own clock in Cat and Bear too), a deep indigo ring over the resource circle's border, shaded like a tube so it reads as a bar and stands apart from the azure mana fill. A mana spend fills it; over the 5 seconds it opens at 12 o'clock and the two ends retreat down both sides to meet at 6 o'clock, leaving it empty. Empty while regenerating (and during a cast: mana is only spent when it lands). (Tried first: a bottom-up "water level" fill, then the reverse of the current look: empty after a spend, with arcs growing from 6 o'clock to meet at 12.) Only casts that really spend mana reset it (shapeshifting does; energy and rage abilities don't): not ones with no mana cost (skinning) or made free by Clearcasting. A cast counts as free only if Clearcasting was up when it was sent and gone just after it landed, because Clearcasting only applies to damage and healing spells: shapeshifting with it up still spends mana. In combat, Clearcasting is only known if Omen of Clarity is tracked in the Cooldown Manager; otherwise a free cast still resets the ring. Doesn't know when mana is full (secret in combat), so it runs then too.
- ~~Cast bar on the #7 ring~~ built: a gold ring replaces the swing ring while casting (fills counter-clockwise from 12 o'clock, via rotated half-ring arcs; falls back to a clockwise swipe if the progress is secret in combat); channels are blue and drain clockwise. Below the ring: `0.0 / 2.5s` (remaining time for channels) and the spell name. Blizzard's player cast bar is hidden by default, since this replaces it (a setting turns that off).
- ~~GCD "Harvey ball" over the big circle~~ built (a faint white pie, 30% alpha, over the resource circle; originally dark). The "scaled to attack speed, for timing shifts to autos" part isn't possible: the timings are secret in combat.
- Indicators: Faerie Fire missing on target; Tiger's Fury off cooldown; Thistle Tea off cooldown; number of shifts affordable with current mana; Enrage available; Enrage buff/debuff active.
- Short cooldowns tracked like DoTs: Tiger's Fury (Faerie Fire, Growl and Primal Bite are built, #9 and #10; add a row to `RINGS` in `CooldownRings.lua`).

## Cooldown widget

A separate box, apart from the HUD, for non-rotational cooldowns (requested 2026-10-01).

- **Choosing:** settings window, Cooldown → Abilities sub-tab. Lists every spellbook spell with a cooldown of its own; tick to track. Tracked ones sit at the top and are dragged up or down to set priority. New ones go last.
- **Showing:** an icon appears only while its ability is on cooldown (not just the GCD) or its buff is up. Visible icons pack together at the chosen alignment point (Layout sub-tab: a 3x3 grid of the box's corners, side middles and centre; default top centre), higher priority nearer the anchor (requested 2026-10-03; replaced plain reading order). Rows fill outward from the anchor's edge, and each row fills outward from the anchor's side. Centre anchors fill a row from its middle, then left, then right, alternating (with an even count, the spot left of centre comes first). Middle anchors fill the middle row, then the row below, then above, alternating. E.g. top centre with 5 in a row: `4 2 1 3 5`, then the second row the same way; bottom right puts priority 1 in the bottom-right corner.
  - On cooldown: greyed-out icon with the time left.
  - Active (our buff on us, or our debuff on the current target, e.g. Growl's taunt or Faerie Fire): the aura's icon in colour (e.g. Elemental Blessing for Skysight), with thin yellow dashes running clockwise around its square border, and the buff's time left. (Yellow dots were tried first; the owner wanted thin lines.)
- **Look:** square icons (round was tried first; the owner preferred squares), sized so every tracked ability fits in the box at once.
- **List:** one entry per spell name (the spellbook can list several ranks; the highest wins). `/catnip spells` shows why any spellbook entry is or isn't offered.
- **Items:** drag an item onto the drop box under the list (Cooldown → Abilities). Anything with a Use: effect (Hearthstone, trinkets) gets its own icon, in the same priority list. Unticking an item removes it.
- **Potions:** all potions share one cooldown, so they share one **Potions** icon, which follows whichever potion was used. Dropping a specific potion (Mighty Rage Potion) is a special case: its buff shows as the Potions icon being active. Unticking Potions forgets the special cases.
- **Cooldown only in some forms:** Faerie Fire has none in caster form but its Cat/Bear version (Faerie Fire (Feral)) does. A spell is listed if any version has a cooldown: the one your form uses now, one seen earlier (remembered in `cdFormCooldowns`), or a known name (Faerie Fire).
- **Form versions:** a spell that changes with your form (Feral Charge becomes Feral Charge (Cat) or (Bear)) is one entry, saved as the base spell. The icon and cooldown follow the version your current form uses, so after a shift it shows that form's charge.
- **Buffs with a different ID:** some abilities give a buff with another name and ID (Skysight, the Skyborne racial, gives Elemental Blessing). Catnip learns these: when a tracked ability is cast out of combat, it looks for a buff of yours that started at that moment and remembers it by ability name. Until then, that ability never shows as active.
- **Moving/resizing:** unlocks with the HUD (Catnip Edit Mode). Drag the box to move it; drag any corner to resize it. Or set exact values in the Cooldown → Layout sub-tab: width, height, and horizontal/vertical position (offset from the screen centre), each a slider with a typeable box.
- **Cooldown only:** Faerie Fire never shows as active, only its cooldown: its ~40s debuff would keep the icon lit long after the 6s Cat/Bear cooldown that matters (`COOLDOWN_ONLY_NAMES` in `Cooldowns.lua`).
- **Limits:** in combat, an ability whose buff is up but which isn't on cooldown can't show (we can't see the buff, only the AuraContainer can). For the same reason, icons with their buff up can't be moved to the front in combat (wanted; every Druid aura is secret then, checked 2026-10-03). The possible route is a separate active zone built from AuraContainer groups, which Blizzard packs itself (see api-research.md). Target debuffs follow the current target only. Countdown numbers are Blizzard's and follow WoW's "Show numbers for cooldowns" option, changed in WoW's own settings (Catnip's checkbox for it was removed: setting a CVar from addon code tainted Blizzard's chat).

## Out of scope

- Utility/movement on the HUD: Dash, Leap, Charge, Bash, War Stomp. (They can go in the cooldown widget.)
- Cooldowns of 1 min or longer: Berserk, potions, Enrage, Nature's Grasp.
- Internal cooldowns: Omen, weapon procs.
