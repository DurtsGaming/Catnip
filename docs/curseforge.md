# Catnip

**A compact HUD for tracking resources, rotation, and cooldowns for Druids in WoW: Forever**

Right now this is mostly built to support feral, although currently work is being done to better support Balance and Resto.

This addon has been developed over the last few weeks of the WoW: Forever Beta. There's still features and customization being actively developed. Expect new versions throughout the rest of the beta, and continued support and development upon release.

If you have any feedback or bug reports, please post on the linked discord!

***

## What it shows

**Resource Tracking**

*   Energy, rage and mana tracker with a five-second rule for regen
*   GCD Tracking
*   How many Cat/Bear shifts before oom, tied into predictive mana cost on cast
*   Omen of Clarity procs
*   Swing timer with maul queueing

**Combo points, DoT durations, Rotation Cooldowns**

*   Combo points tracking
*   Rake and Rip duration timers
*   Cooldowns for rotational abilities like Primal Bite and Shifting Power
*   Omen of Clarity Internal Cooldown Tracker

**Cooldown tracker**

*   A separate widget for the abilities and items you choose (Barkskin, Bash, trinkets, potions, etc)
*   Only shows what's on cooldown or active, in your priority order

***

**Usage**

*   After installing the addon, bring up the menu in game with /catnip

***

**Languages**

Catnip follows your game's language: English, or Simplified Chinese (简体中文), translated by [dodoels](https://github.com/dodoels).

Want Catnip in your language? Translating is just filling in a list of phrases, no coding needed. Ask on the Discord, or see [Translations on GitHub](https://github.com/DurtsGaming/Catnip#translations).
